Edited using the built-in imagegen tool with the project's original sun.png as the reference. Exported to the original 128 x 128 dimensions with ImageMagick; alpha preserved.

- `sun-original.png`: previous texture, retained for comparison or recovery.
- `sun-generated.png`: full-size generated image.

## Prompt

Edit the attached sun sprite for a Minecraft sky renderer. Keep its simple circular, centered, front-facing design, warm yellow-orange palette, and the disk's relative size and position. Change the color inside the sun to a smooth radial gradient: luminous almost-white warm ivory center (approximately RGB 255,252,240), smoothly transitioning through pale buttery yellow to saturated golden yellow and warm orange near the outer rim. The central pale core should cover roughly the inner third of the disk radius, with a gradual continuous transition and no sharp concentric rings, outlines, banding or texture. A subtle soft golden halo fades to fully transparent at the outermost edges of the canvas. Genuine RGBA transparent background outside the glow; no baked black, gray, white or checkerboard backing. Opaque central disk. Preserve alpha transparency. This is a tiny readable 2D game texture, not a realistic solar photograph, no flares, spikes, planets, text, or added objects. Output a square PNG, preferably preserve the original 128x128 dimensions.
